Facilitator sheet

What this session measures

  • G3: on Wednesday boards W1-W4 played in first-handful mode, how often a tester reaches par on the first attempt. Target: 20-60% of first attempts.
  • G4: on relay days (Wednesday and Saturday), how often testers tick guessed. Target: fewer than half of attempts.
  • Preview modes: the same numbers in whole-sowing mode, to compare the two previews. See page 2.

Setup

  • Print the ten board pages for each tester. The answer key is not in this online copy; use your local boards.pdf.
  • Use counters (beans, coins, pebbles) on the drawn houses, or let the tester write the counts as they sow.
  • Order: M1-M3 (warm-up; teach the rules here), then W1-W4, then S1-S3. Do not reveal the answer key between boards.

Rules to read aloud

  1. You take one turn. Your score is the number of shells you put into your ulo during it. Par is the best score possible.
  2. Choose any house in your row (bottom) that has shells. Pick them all up and drop one in each slot along the path: Y0 → … → Y6 → your ulo → L0 → … → L6 → Y0 … Lola's ulo is never sown. Burnt houses are skipped.
  3. Where the last shell lands decides what happens:
    • your ulo: extra turn. You must choose again, from any house of yours with shells.
    • a house (either row) that already had shells: relay. Pick up everything in it and keep sowing from the next slot.
    • an empty house in your row: if the Lola house opposite has shells, capture them and this last shell into your ulo; otherwise nothing happens (a dud). Either way, the turn ends.
    • an empty Lola house: the turn ends.
  4. A handful big enough to go all the way round also drops into the house it came from.
  5. When the turn ends, if Lola's row is empty, every shell left in your row goes to your ulo. (Facilitator note: this cannot happen on any of these ten boards.)

Acting as the preview: first-handful mode

The tester may point at a house and ask what it does before committing. Answer about the first handful only:

  1. Count the shells in the house (N). Starting from the next slot, count N slots along the path. Skip burnt houses and Lola's ulo. The house itself counts if the handful comes all the way round.
  2. Say where the last shell lands and what that means, in one of these forms:
    • “It ends in your ulo: extra turn.”
    • “It ends in Y4, which already has shells: relay.” Stop there.
    • “It ends in your empty Y2; L4 opposite has 3: you'd capture 4.”
    • “It ends in your empty Y2; L4 opposite is empty: dud, the turn ends.”
    • “It ends in Lola's empty L3: the turn ends.”
  3. If the handful passes your ulo, add “… and it drops one in your ulo on the way.”

Never say where a relay goes, whether a move is good, or compare houses. Preview as often as asked. Count it out, or use helper.html (page 2), which gives the exact line to read. Whole-sowing mode is on page 2.

Running each board

  • Up to three attempts. Reset the counters to the printed numbers before each attempt. Stop as soon as the tester reaches par.
  • The tester sows; you check the counting. If a miscount changes the outcome, replay that sowing correctly and note it.
  • After each attempt, ask “Did you work that out, or guess?” and tick the box they choose. Do not suggest an answer.
  • Record the houses in order and the score. helper.html shows the score and whether it reached par.

Report back, per tester

  • W1-W4: par on attempt 1 (yes/no), attempts to par (1, 2, 3 or none).
  • All W and S attempts: the worked it out / partly guessed / guessed tick.
  • Minutes per board, and any rule the tester misunderstood.
  • The tester's group and each board's preview mode (page 2).

Facilitator sheet, page 2: two preview modes

Why two modes

The spike's biggest open question is the preview.

  • First handful: you say only where the first handful ends, and stop at a relay. Counting relays is the tester's job.
  • Whole sowing: you say where the whole sowing ends, relays included, and how many shells it adds to the ulo. The puzzle becomes choosing the order.

Each tester plays every Wednesday and Saturday board in one mode, following the table, so the two modes can be compared.

Groups

Put testers in Group A and Group B alternately as they arrive (A, B, A, B…). Monday boards always use first-handful mode.

In each mode, each group gets one Wednesday board the preview-only bot solves and one it does not.

Using helper.html

  1. Open helper.html (same folder as this pack) in a browser on your laptop. It works offline.
  2. Pick the tester's group and the board. The badge shows the mode for that board, taken from the table.
  3. When the tester points at a house, read that house's line aloud, word for word. Whole-sowing example: “It relays at Y5, then ends in your ulo: extra turn. This sowing adds 1 to your ulo.”
  4. When the tester commits, click Tester sows for that house and let them sow the counters. If the counters come out different from the helper's board, correct the counters.
  5. When the turn ends, the helper shows the score and the houses to write down. Use Start attempt 2 for the next attempt, and Undo last sowing for a mis-click.

Never read the other mode's line, and never say whether a move is good.

Report back, per tester (as well as page 1)

  • The group, and the preview mode ticked on each board page.
  • At the end, ask: “Which boards were more fun: the ones where I told you only the first handful, or the whole sowing? Why?” Write the answer down in their words.
