Paper playtest

Isang Tira

An early test of a daily Sungka puzzle: one turn, and you try to bank as many shells in your ulo as the proven best score. The game itself isn't built yet. This session checks whether the puzzle feels like working it out or like guessing, and which kind of move preview works better.

Play in your browser

All ten boards on your phone or laptop, animated. At the end you copy your results and send them back.

Boards to print

Two facilitator pages and ten boards. Print on A4, one set of boards per tester.

Facilitator helper

Open during the session. Shows the exact preview line to read aloud for each house.

If you're a tester, please don't open the helper. It lets you try every move before committing. The paper answer key isn't online, and the browser version shows the perfect lines only after all ten boards.
